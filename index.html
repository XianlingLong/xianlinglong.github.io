Xianling Long

Assistant Professor of Economics · National School of Development, Peking University

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the National School of Development, Peking University. I received my Ph.D. from Stanford University and hold bachelor's and master's degrees from Peking University.

I work in environmental economics. Recent projects focus on climate change impacts, carbon trading markets, electric vehicles, and energy markets.
